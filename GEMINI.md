# UI & Design Principles

- **Self-Explanatory Design**: Avoid unnecessary secondary labels, helper subtitles, badges, and verbose descriptions. Let the layout do the explaining — good design should explain itself.
- **Minimalist Copy**: Keep titles, labels, and footers ultra-concise. Do not add defensive disclaimers or explanatory sentences when the interactive UI (e.g. search bar, configurator) already makes the purpose obvious.
